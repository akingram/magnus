Football Twitter's Binary Dialect
PWR 2JJA Research-Based Argument (Stanford, Spring 2026). A research paper applying the weak Sapir-Whorf hypothesis to football fandom on X. Argues that platform-engineered vocabulary shapes what fans can think about the sport. Uses Bukayo Saka's Euro 2020 penalty miss as the primary case and Harry Maguire as the racial-variable contrast case. Draws on nine academic sources spanning linguistics, platform studies, and social psychology.
