About

I'm a builder at heart,
a designer by instinct.

I grew up pulling apart websites to see how they worked. That curiosity turned into a career, and now I'm a senior engineer at Stripe, leading UI architecture for the merchant dashboard.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering precision and design intuition. I've partnered with startups, agencies, and Fortune 500 teams to ship products used by millions — always with an obsessive attention to both the pixel and the pipeline.

When I'm not writing code, I'm usually thinking about type, contributing to open source, or writing about interface engineering for the people who read about it obsessively.
