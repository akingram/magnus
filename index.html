Meet Selorm

Sophomore at Stanford,
builder at heart.

I'm a sophomore studying Computer Science (AI track) at Stanford University, with additional study for a minor in Earth Systems. I came to Stanford as an international student from Accra, Ghana, on the Leland Scholarship — a full ride covering tuition, room, board, and meals.

My long-term goal is to launch a climate tech startup at the intersection of AI, machine learning, and human-computer interaction, while building an accelerator for African startups. The work I'm most proud of so far sits at the meeting point of software and social impact: a published iOS app shipped to thousands of users, a Flutter safety app that won a national hackathon, and earlier work upcycling plastic waste into school furniture in Ghana.

I think of education as liberation and self-discovery. My journey from a limited set of high school options in Ghana, through PRESEC's General Arts track, to Stanford's open curriculum mirrors what Plato describes in the Allegory of the Cave.
